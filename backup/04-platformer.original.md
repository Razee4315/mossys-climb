Build a complete pixel-art platformer game called "Mossy's Climb" on HTML5 canvas (no libraries, no image files — draw all sprites in code from pixel arrays or canvas drawing).

Requirements:
- A small frog hero with idle, run, jump and fall animations. Tight controls: coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height, wall slide and wall jump.
- 3 hand-designed levels defined as tile-map strings in the code, getting harder, with a level-complete flag at the end of each.
- Hazards: spikes, moving platforms, crumbling platforms, and patrolling beetle enemies you can stomp.
- Collectible gems with a counter, and checkpoints.
- Smooth camera that follows the player with slight look-ahead, and parallax background layers (hills, trees, clouds).
- Procedural Web Audio sound effects for jump, collect, stomp, death.
- Title screen, level transition screen, and a final victory screen showing total gems and time.
- Crisp pixel scaling (imageSmoothingEnabled = false), fixed-timestep physics, keyboard controls (arrows/WASD + Space) and on-screen touch buttons for mobile.
